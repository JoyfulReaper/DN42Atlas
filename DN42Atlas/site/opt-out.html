Opt out of DN42Atlas

DN42Atlas is a directory and service discovery project, not a vulnerability scanner.

Private preview

DN42Atlas is currently in private preview. Public service listings will not be published before October 16, 2026.

Operators can request complete exclusion during this grace period.

Complete exclusion

An approved complete exclusion means:

  • the hostname or prefix will not be probed in future scans;
  • it will not appear in published Atlas results;
  • once the self-service system is live, it will also be removed from the currently published Atlas without waiting for another crawl.

Hostname rules can cover an exact hostname or its subdomains. Prefix rules can cover IPv4 or IPv6 CIDR ranges.

An authenticated DN42 self-service opt-out system is being developed during the preview period. It is not live yet.

Request exclusion during preview

If you operate a DN42 hostname or address prefix and want it excluded, open an issue in the DN42Atlas repository. Include the hostname or IPv4/IPv6 prefix you operate and want excluded.

Requests are reviewed and applied manually. They are not instantaneous.

robots.txt and page content

To prevent HTTP page-content fetching, place this rule in your robots.txt:

User-agent: DN42Atlas
Disallow: /

robots.txt controls page-content fetching. It does not prevent all probing or listing: Atlas may still request robots.txt and record the origin's reachability. Complete exclusion prevents probing and publication.
