Opt out of DN42Atlas

DN42Atlas discovers publicly reachable HTTP and HTTPS services on DN42 and generates a static service directory.

Complete exclusion

If you operate a DN42 hostname or address prefix and want it excluded, open an issue in the DN42Atlas repository. Include the hostname or IPv4/IPv6 prefix you operate and want excluded. Requests are reviewed and applied manually; submitting an issue does not immediately change the exclusion policy.

Complete exclusions prevent Atlas probing and inclusion in generated results. Hostname rules can cover an exact hostname or its subdomains; prefix rules can cover IPv4 or IPv6 CIDR ranges.

robots.txt and page content

To prevent HTTP page-content fetching, place this rule in your robots.txt:

User-agent: DN42Atlas
Disallow: /

Robots rules control page-content fetching. Atlas may still request robots.txt and record the origin's reachability. Request complete exclusion to prevent probing entirely.
